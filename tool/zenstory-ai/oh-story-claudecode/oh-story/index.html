What it does

Oh Story is a Chinese web-novel writing skill pack with 14 skills. It covers chart scanning, bestseller deconstruction, outlining, long and short drafting, foreshadowing tracking, de-AI-flavor revision, and cover generation. It keeps settings, outlines, chapters and tracking state in files, so a story can continue across sessions. It runs inside the coding agent you already use and needs no separate model setup.

Where it fits your pipeline

It fits the first two steps, idea to story and story to episodes. Its scanning and deconstruction skills study what hooks and pays off in hit stories. Its outlining and drafting skills build hooks, payoff beats and cliffhangers into the text, and its tracking prevents continuity drift across chapters. The deslop skill can reduce robotic phrasing. It writes Chinese web-novel prose, not video scripts or shot lists. Its fit for 1-minute micro-drama episodes is unclear from the README, so you would have to adapt its beats and hooks into episode scripts yourself. It does not touch the reference-image, Kling or ffmpeg steps.

Install

  • npx skills add zenstory-ai/oh-story-claudecode -y -g

How to use

/story-long-write Write chapter 21. Chapter 20 ended on a cliffhanger, so open with a hook and end on a new one.

The skill first writes a detailed outline for the chapter if none exists. It then drafts the chapter text and runs checks for truncated endings and AI-sounding phrasing. Finally it updates the tracking files (foreshadowing list, character states, author-truth and reader-known timelines) from the saved state. Run /story-setup once in the project folder before first use. The README says to open a new session after that.
