What it does

video-use edits existing video footage by conversation. It transcribes speech with word-level timestamps, proposes a plain-English cut plan, then cuts at word boundaries, color grades, adds overlay animations and burns subtitles. It keeps all outputs in an edit folder and remembers decisions across sessions.

Where it fits your pipeline

It slots in after step 4 (Kling clips) and replaces or augments the plain ffmpeg join in step 5. It can improve pacing by trimming silence and filler, tightening cut timing and adding overlays and subtitles. It works from speech, so it fits best when the episode has voiceover or dialogue audio. The confirm-before-cutting step and self-verification may also reduce manual review. The README does not say how well it handles 3-second AI clips without speech.

Install

  • npx -y skills add Pluviobyte/rnskill -g --all

Then copy the skill's folder (skills/video-use) into ~/.claude/skills/.

How to use

Use video-use on my folder of Kling clips and voiceover. Transcribe it, propose a tight 60-second cut with a hook in the first 3 seconds, wait for my approval, then render a preview with burned-in subtitles.

A plain-English cut plan to approve first. After approval you get an edit folder with a cached transcript, a cut list, a master SRT, a preview.mp4 and a final.mp4. Cuts snap to word boundaries with short audio fades at each join.
