What it does

video-talkcraft turns a Chinese voiceover script plus finished narration audio into a Remotion-rendered explainer video. It builds character-level timestamps on CPU, plans a layered SHOTBOOK storyboard, uses a library of 108 motion recipe cards, and runs automated and subagent review loops. It targets talking-head or explainer videos (landscape by default), not Kling-style clip generation.

Where it fits your pipeline

It is mostly a different workflow from the Kling clip pipeline. It fits after a finished script and voiceover, replacing the clip-generation and ffmpeg-join steps with Remotion motion graphics. For a Kling micro-drama it is only partly useful: its sentence-role annotation, hook-first scripting, timing anchors and automated frame review (contact-sheet QA) could improve pacing and cut review time. It is aimed at Chinese-language explainer videos, not scripted drama with cast and wardrobe.

How to use

Use video-talkcraft to turn my Chinese script (script.json) and finished voiceover (audio/full.wav) into a 1920x1080 explainer video with a strong hook in the first sentence.

The skill runs a runtime check, aligns word-level timestamps to your script, annotates each sentence's role, writes a SHOTBOOK storyboard, builds and renders the Remotion video, then runs machine checks and a review pass to fix major issues. You get a rendered video whose visuals follow the narration beats.
