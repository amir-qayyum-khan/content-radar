What it does

video-shotcraft is a Claude Code and Codex skill that builds product promo videos with Remotion. It captures your real pages, picks shots from a library of 150 motion recipes, storyboards the film, cuts it to the beat, adds sound effects and checks frames, then delivers an editable MP4. It is not a generative video model: every frame is drawn by code from your own screenshots and copy.

Where it fits your pipeline

It is built for product promos from screenshots, not for AI-generated drama footage, so it does not slot directly into the Kling reference-to-clip flow. The most useful parts for your pipeline come after step 4 (Kling clips), as an alternative or complement to the ffmpeg join. Its Remotion components (flash cuts, captions, transitions), rhythm and impact shot recipes, beat-synced cutting and Freesound SFX sourcing could add energy to pacing and sound. Its frame-checking step may also help with review. Some adaptation would be needed, because the README does not say it handles Kling clips or story hooks. Treat it as a partial fit.

Install

How to use

Make a 30-second promo for my product using video-shotcraft. Capture my landing page, pick punchy shots, cut to the beat and add sound effects.

A storyboard, then a Remotion project that renders a finished MP4 with shot recipes, transitions, captions and SFX. It can also open a browser editor (Motion Workbench) so you can keep editing the film.
