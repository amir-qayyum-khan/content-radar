What it does

claude-video-vision lets Claude watch and understand video files or YouTube URLs. It extracts frames with ffmpeg and gets timestamped audio transcription from Gemini API, local Whisper, or OpenAI. Claude sees the frames directly and reads the transcript, so it can describe, summarize, or answer questions about a video.

Where it fits your pipeline

Slots in after step 5, when ffmpeg has joined the clips into a 1-minute episode. It can also review single 3-second Kling clips before joining. It helps with the slow manual review step: Claude watches the episode and flags slow stretches, a missing hook, or visual inconsistencies. It does not fix anything or score clips automatically.

Install

  • /plugin install claude-video-vision

How to use

/watch-video episode_03.mp4 "Where does the pacing drag? Note any shot that runs too long, any moment with no visual change, and whether the first 3 seconds have a hook."

Claude extracts frames and audio from the clip. It replies with a timestamped description of what happens on screen and in the audio. It answers your question using that, for example by pointing out slow stretches or a weak opening. This is perception plus Claude's reading of it, not a scored report.
