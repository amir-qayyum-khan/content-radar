What it does

huashu-motion is an agent skill that makes short motion-graphics and promo videos from a one-line request. It reads your product, offers three directions, writes the whole piece, and composes a beat-synced score and sound effects locally. It then opens a browser editor where you click on the frame and comment, and the agent changes only that spot.

Where it fits your pipeline

It fits after step 2 (story to episodes) and can sit beside step 5. It is not a Kling clip generator. Use it for hook or teaser trailers, title and cliffhanger cards, motion-graphic intros, and beat-matched music and SFX. It improves hook, energy and rhythm, because the README says it was tuned against weak openings, flat pacing and a PowerPoint feel. Its click-to-comment editor also cuts down manual review. For the main 1-minute episode, the Kling clips and ffmpeg join still do the work. This tool makes HTML/browser-based animation, not live-action-style footage.

Install

  • npx skills add alchaincyf/huashu-motion

Then copy the skill's folder into ~/.claude/skills/.

How to use

/huashu-motion Make a 30-second vertical teaser for my micro-drama episode. Open with a fast-cut hook in the first 2 seconds, build tension with quick cuts, end on a cliffhanger title card, and score it at 124 BPM with a lift on the reveal.

The agent proposes three directions and then builds the video. It writes a beat-synced score with sound effects on the hits and opens a browser editor. You press C, click on a frame, and write a note, and the agent revises only that element. Exports and an om share pack (GIF plus post copy) come afterwards. Needs Node 22+, Chrome and FFmpeg. The README says it has only been tested on macOS, so behavior on Windows is unknown.
