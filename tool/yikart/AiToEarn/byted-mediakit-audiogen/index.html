What it does

Generates audio with the Seed Audio model (seed-audio-1.0) through a MediaKit MCP proxy. It covers narration, character dialogue, sound effects, ambience and combined audio scenes. Input can be text only, text plus a reference audio or preset voice, or text plus one image. It can also set delivery instructions and return optional word timestamps. It is not for transcription or for deterministic editing, mixing or transcoding.

Where it fits your pipeline

It fits after episodes are scripted and the Kling clips exist or are being timed. It adds dialogue, narration and ambience that the silent 3-second clips lack, and the tone and pacing of the delivery can make the episode feel less robotic. You still have to mix and join the audio with the video in ffmpeg, because this skill does no editing or mixing. How well it works with your pipeline depends on the MediaKit setup. Then copy the skill's folder (skills/kimi-plugin-aitoearn/skills/aitoearn-growth/references/byted-mediakit-audiogen) into ~/.claude/skills/.

How to use

Generate a 20-second MP3 voiceover for episode 3 of my micro-drama. Use this exact line: "You lied to me. Again." Delivery: low, shaking voice, a held breath before "Again", with tension building. Add a faint rain ambience track as a separate clip.

Separate audio files, one per requested clip or variant. They should be MP3 at 44100 Hz by default, with the supplied dialogue kept word for word. The delivery follows your description, and a word-timestamp option is available if you ask for it. If the MediaKit proxy is unavailable, the skill reports that instead of working around it.
