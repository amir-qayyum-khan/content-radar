What it costs

The README doesn't say what it costs.

What it does

youtube-data lets Claude fetch structured YouTube data through the TranscriptAPI.com service, without Google API quotas or OAuth. It can pull timestamped transcripts, video metadata (views, likes, tags, duration), search results, channel upload lists and playlist contents. It needs a TRANSCRIPT_API_KEY. The readme says a free account includes 100 credits, and most calls cost 1 credit.

Where it fits your pipeline

Use it before step 1 (idea to story) and step 2 (story to episodes) as research. Pull transcripts and view stats from hit micro-dramas and shorts, then study their hooks, line pacing and cliffhanger placement. This gives you concrete benchmarks to counter slow, robotic scripts. It doesn't generate or review clips.

Install

  • /plugin marketplace add hashgraph-online/awesome-codex-plugins

Then copy the skill's folder (plugins/ZeroPointRepo/youtube-skills/skills/youtube-data) into ~/.claude/skills/.

How to use

Find the 10 most-viewed videos on the YouTube channel @ReelShort, pull the transcripts of the top 3, and tell me how each one opens in the first 10 seconds and where the cliffhanger lands.

A list of the channel's most popular videos with view counts, the timestamped transcripts of the top 3, and a short breakdown of each one's opening line, beat timing and ending hook. Claude will ask for your TRANSCRIPT_API_KEY first if it isn't set.
