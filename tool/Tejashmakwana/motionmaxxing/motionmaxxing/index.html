What it does

motionmaxxing is a skill that makes Claude act as a motion director for motion-graphics films such as launch videos, product promos, kinetic-type pieces and social ads. It finds an idea specific to the brand, builds a visual world, and animates it with a seek-safe GSAP runtime using timing curves measured from 53 professional recreations. It checks its own render with scripted gates (look.py, lint.mjs) instead of self-certifying. Given a website URL it captures the brand's colours, fonts, logo and copy. With an ElevenLabs key it can also write and voice a script and cut picture and sound together.

Where it fits your pipeline

It is not a core drama step. It fits best as a side tool after story-to-episodes (step 2) for making series trailers, promo stings, title cards, cold-open kinetic-type hooks or social ads. It improves pacing with cut-on-motion, hold-before-the-point and hand-off rules, and its scripted review gates cut some manual review time. It does not replace Kling clips or the ffmpeg episode join.

How to use

Use motionmaxxing to make a 15-second promo film for my micro-drama series. Find a hook idea only this show could own, plan each beat around what it makes the viewer believe, then build and review it.

A project folder (e.g. film/) with a brand-specific idea, a beat-by-beat plan where each beat buys one belief, an HTML/GSAP film rendered with ffmpeg and Chrome, and a NOTE.md with gate results from look.py and lint.mjs. It targets motion graphics, promos and trailers, not live-action or Kling-style generated drama clips.
