What it does

rea-video reverse-engineers a video shot by shot. It judges whether each shot was live action, AI-generated, 3D, code-rendered or screen capture. It also breaks down captions, cuts, audio and music, and writes a remake plan. Output is an HTML/PDF report for you and a layered VIDEO.md file for AI agents.

Where it fits your pipeline

Use it before step 2 (story to episodes) and step 3 (reference images), on successful reference micro-dramas. It shows the hook timing, cut rhythm and shot variety of videos that already work. You can copy that structure into your beat sheets and your 3-second Kling clip plan. It does not generate story or clips itself. It improves pacing by giving you a measurable benchmark. It may also speed up review, because you can compare your episode's cut rhythm against a reference. That second point is an inference, not a documented feature.

How to use

Break down this video shot by shot and give me a remake plan for Kling: /path/to/reference-drama-episode.mp4. Focus on cut timing, hook in the first 3 seconds, and where tension rises.

A report (report.html/PDF) and a VIDEO.md package. They list each shot and whether it is live, AI, 3D or code-made. They also cover cut points and speed, captions with timings, voice and music with BPM and sound-effect placement, and a remake plan. The agent installs the CLI on first run, which needs ffmpeg and Python 3.10+.
