What it does

Lemo-Opuscar is a library of 99 film styles that a coding agent follows to make a short film entirely in code. It renders canvas and WebGL pages frame by frame, scores the film from free sample libraries, and adds text-to-speech narration, with no video generation or stock footage. You pick a style, give a topic in one sentence, and the agent produces a finished video.

Where it fits your pipeline

It does not fit neatly into the Kling-plus-ffmpeg pipeline, because it makes films from code-rendered graphics rather than from reference images or generated clips. It is most useful as a side track: stylised teasers, title cards, recap or explainer pieces, or mood tests for an episode. It can also help with pacing, since a storyboard step and narration and score are built in. It will not produce live-action-looking character drama like Kling does, and it does not fix hooks or tension in the story itself.

Install

  • /plugin marketplace add lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar

Then copy the skill's folder into ~/.claude/skills/.

How to use

Make a 45-second film in the Neon Tubes style about a young woman who discovers her landlord has been secretly reading her messages. Tense female narrator.

The agent first asks a few questions: whether you have your own voice or music, and whether you want a storyboard first. It then writes code to draw, score and narrate the film, and returns a finished 1920x1080, 24 fps video in the folder you started from. A film takes roughly 30 to 60 minutes and a fair number of tokens.
