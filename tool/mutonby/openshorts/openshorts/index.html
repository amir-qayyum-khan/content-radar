What it does

OpenShorts is an open-source (MIT) platform that turns long videos into 9:16 short clips. It finds strong moments, reframes with face tracking, adds word-level captions and hook overlays, and gives each clip a virality score and platform titles. It also has an AI UGC actor video tool, a YouTube Studio for titles and thumbnails, and auto-posting through Upload-Post.

Where it fits your pipeline

Slots in after step 5 (ffmpeg joins clips into a 1-minute episode), in the captions-publish stage. It adds captions, hook overlays, 9:16 reframing, titles and scheduled posting for teasers or cut-downs of finished episodes. It does not fix story or pacing upstream. Its virality scores might speed up review a little, but the README only shows them for long-form talk content.

How to use

Take my finished 1-minute episode file, pick the strongest 15-30 second moments, reframe them to 9:16, burn in captions and write a hook line for each teaser clip.

A set of captioned 9:16 clips, each with a hook overlay, a virality score and platform titles. The clips open in an editor where you can adjust cuts and framing and re-render. The README does not say how well this works on scripted, AI-generated drama rather than podcasts and interviews.
