What it does

OpenMotion is an open-source agentic video studio that recreates a reference video (an uploaded file or a social link) using your own brand kit and copy. Your agent breaks the reference into shots, timing and transitions, writes them as HTML plus a Motion.md file, and generates missing assets such as images, music and voice through your own provider keys. You can adjust timing in Studio and render an MP4 from the same project.

Where it fits your pipeline

It slots in after step 2 (story to episodes) and alongside or after step 3 (reference images). Use it to build trailers, hooks, title cards and recap or teaser cuts, and to borrow the pacing of fast reference videos through Motion.md shot timing and transitions. It could help with the slow, robotic pacing by giving you a shot-by-shot timing and transition blueprint to copy, but it is built around motion-graphics and brand-style video recreation. It does not replace Kling clip generation or the ffmpeg join for live-action-style drama episodes, and the README does not mention automated review or QC.

How to use

Use OpenMotion to connect Cloud MCP, then help me recreate this fast-paced 20-second teaser as a vertical micro-drama trailer with a cold-open hook, quick cuts, a tension build and a cliffhanger end card. Reference: <paste video link>

The agent connects to OpenMotion Cloud MCP through OAuth. It breaks the reference down into a shot-by-shot Motion.md covering order, timing and transitions, and builds an HTML-based video project. Missing assets are generated through your own provider keys. You can then tweak timing in Studio and render an MP4.
