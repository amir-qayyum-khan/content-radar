What it does

A story-structuring skill that turns a loose idea into production-ready beats for short dramas, trailers, or adaptations. It locks a narrative engine (desire, pressure, turning point, payoff or cliffhanger), opens on conflict in the first 3 seconds, and requires every beat to raise stakes, reveal information, or reverse power. It also keeps names, lore, and aspect ratio stable across beats. It ships inside the NolanX repo and is built for NolanX's own agents (planner, script_writer, video_designer, flf_video_designer).

Where it fits your pipeline

It slots in at step 1-2, between the idea and the episode scripts. It targets the slow, robotic pacing problem by forcing a hook, escalating stakes beat by beat, and cliffhanger endings before any reference images or Kling clips are made. Its beat structure of 3-7 dense beats also maps well onto 3-second clips. It is a prompt-style skill meant for NolanX's agents, so it may need adapting (for example, pasting the rules into a Claude Code skill or prompt) for a Kling and ffmpeg pipeline. I could not confirm a standalone Claude Code plugin install from the README.

Then copy the skill's folder (skills/cinematic-story-architecture) into ~/.claude/skills/.

How to use

Using the cinematic-story-architecture approach, turn this idea into a 1-minute vertical micro-drama episode of 5 beats: a wife finds her husband's second phone ringing during their anniversary dinner. Open on the ringing phone, escalate every beat, and end on a cliffhanger. Keep the character names Mira and Daniel.

A named beat list of 3-7 beats. Each beat gives the action, emotional turn, visual revelation, camera intention, and the purpose of any dialogue or silence. The first beat is a hook in the first 3 seconds, tension rises through the middle, and the episode ends on a twist or unresolved danger. Names, world details, and 9:16 format stay consistent across all beats.
