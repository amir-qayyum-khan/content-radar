What it does

scenario-storyboards turns a story into a sequence of consistent Scenario images, such as comic pages, storybooks, shot lists or pre-viz frames. It scripts each panel as a shot list, locks the cast with an approved reference hero, then runs one generation per panel with identical style, character and setting lines. Dialogue is lettered afterward so text stays legible, and approved panels can feed image-to-video.

Where it fits your pipeline

Slots in after Story -> episodes and covers step 3 (reference images: stills, cast, locations). It improves pacing by planning each episode as coverage (master shot, size ladder as tension rises, inserts and reactions) rather than a flat run of pictures. It keeps characters consistent across panels, so approved panels can be used as first frames for Kling clips and rejected ones are fixed before costly video generation.

Install

  • npx skills add scenario-labs/skills

Then copy the skill's folder (skills/scenario-storyboards) into ~/.claude/skills/.

How to use

Storyboard episode 3 of my micro-drama as 8 panels: a master shot of the apartment, then closer shots as the heroine finds the letter, ending on a cliffhanger insert. Keep her face and costume the same in every panel and keep dialogue out of the art.

A shot-list table (panel, shot size, angle, lens, action, dialogue), an approved hero reference with a written character baseline, and eight separately generated, text-free panels matching that hero. Dialogue is lettered in post. Single panels can be re-run, and approved ones can serve as first frames for video generation.
