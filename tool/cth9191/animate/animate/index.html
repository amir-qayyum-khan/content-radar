What it does

Animate is a Claude Code skill that makes short animated videos entirely in code. Each piece is drawn and scored procedurally, rendered frame by frame with a headless browser, and exported to MP4 with ffmpeg. It has built-in styles (cut paper, crosshatch ink, riso, sketchbook, math, pixel, isometric) or can match a new look from your references. It can also add voice-over, cut to your music, and export 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9.

Where it fits your pipeline

It is a separate route from the Kling reference-image pipeline, not a drop-in step. It fits as an alternative to steps 3-5 for animated, explainer or stylised episodes, or for stylised intros, recap cards and transitions. It improves pacing through a story grammar with hooks, silence before the payoff and a loud payoff, beat-synced cuts to your music, and voice-timed picture. Its built-in review tools (contact sheets, text and loudness checks) cut manual review time. It does not generate photoreal live-action-style drama clips, and the README does not show it handling character-driven dramatic scenes. Needs Node 18+, ffmpeg on PATH and Playwright Chromium. Voice-over also needs faster-whisper and optionally the ElevenLabs connector.

Install

  • /plugin marketplace add cth9191/animate
  • /plugin install animate@animate

Then copy the skill's folder into ~/.claude/skills/.

How to use

/animate a 45-second history of the bicycle, cut paper, vertical 9:16

Claude asks a few questions and has you approve three cheap previews first. It then builds a piece under pieces/<name>/ and exports an MP4. Review contact sheets and automated checks for cut-off text and loudness are available.
