What it does

Brag turns the project you are working in into a short, shareable launch video with music, motion and share copy. It ships two skills: brag, which uses the Hyperframes renderer, and brag-slim, a lean single-file version for newer Claude models with no Hyperframes dependency. Output lands in a brag-output folder with a plan, composition brief, share copy and a rendered brag.mp4.

Where it fits your pipeline

It is a side tool, not a core step of the micro-drama pipeline. It builds promo videos for software projects from a codebase or site, not drama episodes from Kling clips. At best it could help make a teaser or launch video for a drama channel or app, after the episodes are done. It does not fix story pacing, hooks or review. For stage fit, it belongs in other video/audio, or loosely in captions-publish for promo and share copy.

Install

  • /plugin marketplace add latent-spaces/brag
  • /plugin install brag@brag

How to use

let's /brag about this project, with a tense, fast-paced trailer tone

A brag-output folder containing a plan, a composition brief, share copy and a rendered brag.mp4 launch video with music and motion. Voiceover is off unless you pass --voice.
